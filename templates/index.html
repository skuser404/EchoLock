🔐 EchoLock

AI-Based Behavioral Biometric Authentication using Typing Pattern Analysis

🛡️ Welcome to EchoLock

EchoLock uses advanced machine learning to analyze your unique typing patterns. Unlike traditional passwords, your typing rhythm is nearly impossible to replicate, providing an additional layer of security.

How it works:

  • Registration: Enter your PIN 5 times to create your biometric profile
  • Authentication: Type your PIN naturally - we analyze your typing pattern
  • Security: Even if someone knows your PIN, they can't mimic your typing style
